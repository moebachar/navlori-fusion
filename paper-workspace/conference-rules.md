# ICINCO 2026 - submission rules (distilled)
Source: https://icinco.scitevents.org/Guidelines.aspx (verified May 2026)
Venue: Angers, France, 26-28 October 2026.

## Track
Regular Paper (completed + validated). 10,000-50,000 chars excl. spaces. After review: Full (12pp) or Short (8pp).
Alternative: Position Paper, 8,000-40,000 chars, Short only. Pick Regular unless decided otherwise.

## Second-round deadlines
Submission 26 Jun 2026; Notification 31 Jul 2026; Camera-ready 8 Sep 2026. Submitted via PRIMORIS as PDF.

## Hard rules
- Double-blind: no names, affiliations, lab/project name, grants, self-revealing acks. Cite prior self-work in 3rd person.
- AI disclosure: any AI-generated text must be disclosed in Acknowledgements (camera-ready) with a citation to the AI system. Maintain paper-workspace/ai-usage.md.
- <20% self-citations.
- No plagiarism / no double submission.
- Length counted in CHARACTERS EXCLUDING SPACES, not pages.

## Reviewer rating
Relevance, Originality, Technical Quality, Significance, Presentation. Plus: Abstract+Intro adequate? More experiments? Comparative eval? Critical discussion? Figures adequate? Conclusions/Future Work convincing? References up-to-date? Formatting OK? English OK?

## Format (full rules in paper-format skill)
SCITEPRESS LaTeX, A4, two columns, 10pt body. APA author-year (\cite{key}); never numeric [1]. Section ALL-CAPS; sub/subsub titlecase. Captions ABOVE tables, BELOW figures, 9pt, period at end. No page numbers, no headers/footers.
